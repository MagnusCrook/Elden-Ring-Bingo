    How Elden Ring Bingo works

    Two players race through Elden Ring at the same time, trying to complete the challenges on a shared 5×5 bingo card. The first to complete a line wins.

    Setting up

    1. On the Play page, choose Early game, Midgame or Late game and press Generate card until you both like the board. You can edit the challenge pools first if you want to tailor them.
    2. Once the card is chosen, each player starts a new playthrough of Elden Ring.
    3. As you complete challenges, mark them on the card. Gold is player 1 and blue is player 2.

    The goal

    Be the first player to mark five squares in a row, whether across, down or diagonally. That is a bingo, and it wins the game.

    The catch

    If the other player has marked a square, you can’t mark that square for yourself.

    Every square belongs to whoever completes it first, so shared challenges become a race. Claiming a square also blocks the lines your opponent was building through it.

    If nobody gets a bingo

    If neither player can complete a line, keep playing and claim as many squares as you can. The game only ends on count once every square has been marked by one player or the other, and then the winner is whoever has marked the most squares. A tied count is a draw, so settle it with a rematch.

    Using this site

    The site announces bingos and finished games with a pop-up. You can save cards you like and reopen them later, and add or remove challenges in each phase’s pool.
